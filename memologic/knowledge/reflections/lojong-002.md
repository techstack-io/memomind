retrieval_summary: |
  See Experience as a Dream

  Use this reflection when someone is fixated on a situation as though it
  were permanent, solid, and the only thing that exists — consumed by a
  problem, a worry, or an emotional state to the point where nothing else
  feels real or accessible. Common patterns include catastrophizing,
  clinging to a fixed version of events, and rumination that keeps
  circling the same fixed point.

  The central teaching is not that pain is fake or that experience doesn't
  matter. It's that the mind's habit of treating a passing state as solid,
  permanent, and all-encompassing is itself a distortion — one worth
  noticing directly. A dream can be vivid, frightening, or wonderful
